What we handle

Support for every step of the journey.

Applications & registration

Collect applications, review submitted details and keep eligibility, documents and payment information organized.

Clear team responsibilities

Assessment preparation

Evaluation & selection

Results & next steps

FAQ

Questions we get every week.

Can you help organize our existing applicant information?

Yes. Tell us where your information is currently kept, and we'll help plan a practical way to bring it together and review it.

Can you support assessments and selection?

Yes. We can help coordinate assessment schedules, organize reviews, apply your criteria and keep shortlisting decisions clear.

Can different reviewers work together?

Yes. We help teams assign reviews, share relevant information and keep track of progress.

Can you help with results and certificates?

Yes. We can help prepare outcome updates and certificates and make sure participants know what to do next.

Will someone guide us through the process?

Yes. Our team works with you from the initial planning conversation through your program and its follow-up.

Can support be tailored to our organization?

Yes. We'll learn about your needs and recommend the right level of help for your team and participants.
