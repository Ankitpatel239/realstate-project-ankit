Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How do I book a service?
Simply browse our services, select the one you need, choose a convenient time slot, and confirm your booking. You'll receive instant confirmation and your pro's details.
Are your professionals insured and background-checked?
Absolutely. Every professional on our platform undergoes a rigorous background check, license verification, and is fully insured. Your safety and peace of mind are our top priorities.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 4 hours before your appointment. For Standard and Premium members, cancellation is free anytime.
How is pricing determined?
Prices are based on the type of service, scope of work, and your location. You'll see the exact price before confirming your booking — no hidden fees, ever.
What if I'm not satisfied with the service?
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, contact our support team within 24 hours and we'll arrange a redo or provide a full refund.
