A few quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Here’s what to know before exploring the CodeGen workspace.

What can I create with CodeGen?

CodeGen is designed to help organize QR codes, barcodes, and campaigns, with workspace views for scan activity and performance.

Can I use the sample codes as real codes?

No. The QR graphic and dashboard figures on this landing page are illustrations. The linked workspace is currently a prototype with sample data, so it does not create production-ready scannable codes.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is needed to explore these pages. Use the Open workspace links to view the CodeGen prototype in your browser.

How secure is the platform?

All redirects are SSL‑encrypted, and we enforce anti‑abuse protections. Enterprise plans include SOC2 and GDPR compliance with dedicated support.
